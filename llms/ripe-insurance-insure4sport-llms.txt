[Fitness Instructor Insurance](https://www.insure4sport.co.uk/fitness-instructor-insurance/): Specialist insurance for fitness instructors teaching in gyms, studios, or privately, with options for liability, personal accident, and equipment.
[Players Insurance](https://www.insure4sport.co.uk/players-insurance/): Financial protection for amateur and professional players against injury, loss of earnings, and equipment damage.
[Sports Coaching Insurance](https://www.insure4sport.co.uk/sports-coaching-insurance/): Insurance for coaches across multiple sports, providing liability, professional indemnity, and personal accident cover.
[Sports Team Insurance](https://www.insure4sport.co.uk/sports-team-insurance/): Insurance for teams and clubs of all sizes, including financial protection for players, coaches, equipment, and liability.
