# Ripe Insurance

> Ripe Insurance Services Limited (trading as Ripe, part of Ripe Thinking Limited) is a
> Stockport-based UK digital Managing General Agent and specialist insurance intermediary,
> authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FRN 313411). Founded in 1997
> and majority-backed by Aquiline Capital Partners, it sells niche personal and
> small-commercial lines direct to consumers through its own brands — Cycleplan, Golf Care,
> Insure4Boats, Insure4Sport and Ripe Caravans — and reports more than 400,000 policyholders.

> API posture: partner-gated, no public developer surface. There is no developer portal, no
> self-serve API programme, and no published quote, bind, issue or FNOL API. The only
> anonymously reachable machine-readable API on the estate is the Umbraco CMS Content
> Delivery API that ships with the .NET content management system behind the brand websites —
> read-only content and media, infrastructure rather than an insurance product API. Ripe's
> real agent-facing investment is elsewhere: three of its brands publish llms.txt, and its
> April 2026 Cycleplan ChatGPT app delivers real-time cycling quotes conversationally with no
> public API, MCP server or specification behind it.

## APIs

- [Ripe Insurance Umbraco Content Delivery API](https://www.ripeinsurance.co.uk/umbraco/swagger/index.html): Read-only Umbraco CMS content and media. Eight GET operations over v2. Anonymous. Base URL https://www.ripeinsurance.co.uk/umbraco/delivery/api/v2. Not an insurance API.
- [Cycleplan Umbraco Content Delivery API](https://www.cycleplan.co.uk/umbraco/swagger/index.html): The same surface on the Cycleplan brand, one CMS version behind — sixteen operations because the legacy v1 paths are still enabled.

## Notes for agents

- There is no public insurance API. Do not attempt to quote, bind, issue or notify a claim programmatically — those journeys exist only in the first-party web funnels and, for cycling cover, the Cycleplan ChatGPT app.
- The Content Delivery API is anonymous but undocumented by Ripe and excluded from crawling (robots.txt "Disallow: /umbraco/"). Treat it as incidental infrastructure, and respect the exclusion.
- The richest machine-readable description of what Ripe actually sells is the brand llms.txt files listed above, not the API.
